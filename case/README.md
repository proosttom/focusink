# FocusInk case

A case for the Waveshare ESP32-S3-ePaper-1.54 with a 45 × 30 × 8 mm LiPo, printed in PETG. Outside size: 40.3 × 62.3 × 26.9 mm.

It's styled like an old terminal. A wedge-shaped base is built into the bottom: it's deepest at the front, slightly narrower than the case and set off by a groove. With the base flat on the desk, the screen tilts up 15° towards you.

| File | Part | Print orientation |
|---|---|---|
| `focus_case_body.stl` | Front and walls, display window and guide rails, flex buttons, thick end walls with the 2 screw holes, wedge base with USB-C tunnel | Window side down |
| `focus_case_carrier.stl` | Plate the battery lies on; 4 legs press the PCB at its mounting holes | Plate down |
| `focus_case_lid.stl` | Screw-on back cover: locating lip, battery end stops, 2 countersunk holes | Outer face down |

All three are already oriented for printing and need no supports.

**You also need:** 2 × M2 × 6 mm countersunk screws, self-tapping (for plastic) or ordinary machine screws; both cut their own thread in the 1.7 mm pilot holes.

## Kobra S1, PETG

Open **`focus_case.3mf`** in Anycubic Slicer Next. It has all three parts on one plate and is already set up so the walls print solid. Estimate: about 51 min and 23 g of PETG.

It uses the stock "0.20mm Standard @Anycubic Kobra S1 0.4 nozzle" profile and stock Anycubic PETG, with these changes:

| Setting | Stock | Here | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wall loops | 2 | 5 | The stock 2 loops are only ~0.85 mm, so the rest of the 1.8 mm wall was infill. 5 loops fill it completely. |
| Wall generator | Classic | Arachne | Varies the line width so the loops fill each wall exactly, with no gaps. |
| Top / bottom shell layers | 5 / 3 | 8 / 8 | Makes the 1.5 mm front and the 1.6 mm lid solid. |
| Gap fill | Top/bottom | Everywhere | Fills the slivers around the buttons and screw holes. |
| Sparse infill | 15 % | 25 % | Only the inside of the wedge base still gets infill. |
| Prime tower | On | Off | Single colour. |

If you regenerate the STLs, import them into the 3MF again (or set the same values yourself).

- Print the body on the textured PEI side if you want a matte front.
- Slow the outer wall down if the 0.6 mm button slots come out closed.

## Buttons

The side buttons are cantilever tabs cut into the wall. Each tab has a small nub inside that presses the switch.

- **PWR** (lower, near the USB end): raised ▶‖ icon. Start or pause the current task.
- **BOOT** (upper): raised ✓ icon. Complete the task.

## Assembly

1. Put the display face down into the body. Shallow rails on both long sides and the top end centre the glass behind the window. The window is 28.6 × 29.6 mm, a bit larger than the 27 mm active area, so the top and bottom rows aren't cut off. There's no rail at the bottom because the USB-C connector sits there.
2. Seat the PCB on the 4 standoffs. The small pins go into the PCB's corner mounting holes. It should drop in without force: there's about 0.6 mm of room on every side and nothing sticks into the opening.
3. Put the carrier on top, legs on the same 4 holes. It has 0.5 mm of room on every side.
4. Plug in the battery. Route the lead through the slot in the middle of the carrier or the notch at either end. Lay the battery on the plate.
5. Put the lid on (the lip drops into the opening) and drive in the 2 screws, one in the middle of each end. Stop when they're snug: PETG strips if you overtighten.

To charge, lay it on its back. The USB-C port is reached through a tunnel in the base, from underneath.

Nothing is clamped: the battery has 0.8 mm of headroom under the lid, so pressing the lid doesn't push the PCB or the buttons. If it rattles, a 0.5 mm foam pad on the battery takes up the slack.

## Changing it

Everything is generated by `focus_case.py`. The parameters are at the top: battery size, wall thickness, `FIT`, `TILT_DEG` (screen tilt), `BASE_INSET`, `WALL_END` (end walls that hold the screws).

Regenerate the STLs and `focus_case.FCStd`:

```bash
/Applications/FreeCAD.app/Contents/Resources/bin/freecadcmd focus_case.py
```

You can also open `focus_case.py` in FreeCAD as a macro and run it.

- Lid lip too tight or too loose: change `FIT` (0.2 per side by default).
- Screws too tight or loose: change `PILOT_D` (1.7 by default).
- Buttons: `NUB_GAP` is the gap between the button nub and the switch (0.35). Lower it if the buttons need too much travel, raise it if they're pressed without being touched.
- Battery headroom: `BAT_GAP` (0.8).
- Different battery: change `BAT_L`, `BAT_W`, `BAT_T`. The case depth follows `BAT_T`.

Board geometry comes from Waveshare's STEP model (`04_Hardware/Dimensions`). The parts were checked against it with no collisions between board, battery and case.
